The Format

How It's Scored

Three matches, each a two-on-two fourball (best ball) — Notre Dame pair vs. Saratoga pair. On every hole, each player plays their own ball; the better of the two scores on each team counts. Whichever team has the lower ball wins the hole. Match the other side, and the hole is halved.

Each match is worth one point toward the Wurl Cup — a halved match splits the point, ½ to each side. A match can finish early if one team goes up by more holes than remain to be played (a "3&2" finish, for example). Whichever side claims the most points across all three matches wins the Cup. Tied at 1½ apiece, the Cup is shared — and Kieran buys the next round.
